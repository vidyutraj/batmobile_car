Line Motor

Connecting…
— coast
VOUT3 is the centre sensor, VOUT4 the outer one. Their pattern picks a state, and the motor runs at that state's speed (0 = stop). A sensor flips black/white only 50 mV past its threshold. Auto is always off after a reset.

OLED display

—
Waiting for data…
128×64 SSD1306 at I2C 0x3C · SDA GPIO21 · SCL GPIO22 · VCC 3V3 · GND. Mirrors the screen: BLACK when VOUT3 or VOUT4 sees black. The ESP32 checks for the display every 2 s.

Piezo

—
PAM8904 piezo driver: SIG on GPIO23, VIN 5V, GND, VO+/VO− to the piezo (either way round). 2 kHz over white, 4 kHz over black, following the same WHITE/BLACK as the OLED. M mute

Paused
Move the array over your board and press W while it is over white, B while the line is under it, P to pause. Each sensor's threshold is fitted between its white readings and the dark readings it gave while labelled black, so sweep the line under every sensor. A sensor that never went clearly darker than its white keeps its current threshold.
